3 · Describing data

Distributions, summaries, correlation

Author

Eduard (Eddie) Bukin

Before fitting anything, look at the data. This topic covers summarising and plotting numerical and categorical variables, and measuring how two variables move together — along with the ways correlation can mislead you.
